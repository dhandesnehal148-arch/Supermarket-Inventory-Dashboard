# 🛒 Supermarket Inventory & Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Supermarket Inventory & Sales Dashboard** is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The purpose of this project is to analyze supermarket sales performance, monitor inventory levels, and understand product-wise sales trends through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard helps users explore business data, identify sales patterns, and gain insights that can support better inventory management and business decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall supermarket sales performance.
- Monitor inventory levels and product availability.
- Understand product-wise sales trends.
- Identify high-performing products and categories.
- Track important business metrics using KPI cards.
- Present business data through interactive charts and visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
- Power Query for data transformation
- DAX for calculations and measures, where applicable
- Data modeling and relationships
- Interactive charts, slicers, and KPI visuals

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
Analyze sales trends and compare sales performance across available categories, products, or time periods.

### 2. Inventory Analysis
Explore inventory quantities and product availability to support inventory monitoring.

### 3. Product-Wise Analysis
Identify products that contribute to sales and compare their performance.

### 4. Category-Wise Analysis
Compare sales performance across product categories, where category data is available.

### 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Display important metrics such as total sales, sales quantity, and inventory levels, depending on the measures included in the dashboard.

### 6. Interactive Dashboard
Use slicers and filters to explore the data and analyze specific products, categories, or periods.

## 🔍 Business Benefits

- Helps monitor sales performance.
- Supports inventory planning and stock management.
- Makes product and category comparisons easier.
- Helps identify sales trends and potential business opportunities.
- Presents key information in a clear and understandable format.

## 🛠️ Project Workflow

1. Import the dataset into Power BI Desktop.
2. Clean and transform the data using Power Query.
3. Create data relationships and calculations where required.
4. Develop visualizations and KPI measures.
5. Design the interactive dashboard.
6. Analyze the results and summarize business insights.
